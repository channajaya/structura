10.0 Worked-example route

The chapter uses the same calculation-sheet format as earlier worked examples. The main design logic is to calculate the applied design bending moment and compare it with the design flexural moment resistance.

1 · PressureConvert characteristic wind pressure to design lateral pressure.
2 · MomentCalculate MEd = αWEdh².
3 · ResistanceCalculate MRd = fxd1Z.
4 · CheckConfirm MEd ≤ MRd.
Design reminder: the values used here are for teaching. For real projects, verify wind pressure, flexural strength, material partial factors and moment coefficients from the project National Annex and current design documents.

10.1 Worked Example WE-07 — Simply supported laterally loaded panel

Given a 215 mm thick single-leaf clay masonry panel, 2.7 m high and 3.0 m long, simply supported along top and bottom and laterally loaded by wind. Use M6 mortar, Category I units with Class 2 execution for flexural tension, and fxk1 = 0.40 N/mm².

10.2 Worked Example WE-08 — Panel with a window opening

Given a 215 mm thick single-leaf wall panel, 4.0 m long and 2.7 m high, containing a 1.5 m wide by 1.2 m high window. The sill is 0.9 m above the base. Use the same wind pressure and moment resistance from WE-07.

WE-08 subdivision visual

Professor’s note: this is a teaching subdivision method. If the opening is large, close to supports, or the panel has irregular boundary conditions, use first-principles yield-line analysis or FEM and explicitly model the anisotropic masonry properties.

Common mistakes to avoid

Using characteristic wind pressure Wk directly instead of design pressure WEd.
Using the wrong flexural strength direction. For this one-way vertical spanning example, fxk1 is used.
Assuming an opening is only an architectural detail. It can change the effective structural panel.
Forcing an irregular wall into a simple rectangular panel when yield-line or FEM analysis is more appropriate.

10.3 Chapter summary

WE-07

The direct flexural-strength check for the simply supported wind-loaded panel gives MEd = 0.820 kNm/m and MRd = 1.14 kNm/m. The panel passes.

WE-08

The panel with an opening is conservatively subdivided. The side-pier check governs, with MEd,crit = 0.820 kNm/m. The wall passes under the teaching assumptions.

Key lesson: the controlling check is not always at the opening. In this example, the side pier remains critical because it spans the full wall height.
